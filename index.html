About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). My research interests lie in 3D computer vision, with a focus on depth estimation and point cloud tracking.

I am currently working on depth estimation for fisheye imagery. My previous research experience focuses on 3D single-object tracking, including BEV representations, multi-scale feature fusion, and temporal modeling for sparse and occluded point clouds.

For research discussions, feel free to get in touch.
